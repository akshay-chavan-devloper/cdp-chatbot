To integrate your data with Zeotap: 
 
1.	Navigate to the "Integrations" section in the Zeotap dashboard. 
 
2.	Choose your data source (e.g., CRM, website, app). 
 
3.	Configure the API keys and authentication
4.	Map your data fields to Zeotap's schema. 
 
5.	Save the integration, and your data will be synced with Zeotap for use in campaigns.
