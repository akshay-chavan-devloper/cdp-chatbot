To create a user profile in mParticle: 
 
1.	Log in to the mParticle dashboard. 
 
2.	Navigate to the "Profiles" tab. 
 
3.	Click "Create New Profile". 
 
4.	Fill out the required information such as the user ID, attributes, etc. 
 
5.	Save the profile, and it will be available for use in your data streams and integrations.
